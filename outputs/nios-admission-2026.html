Who can apply?

Learners seeking Secondary or Senior Secondary certification can explore NIOS subject combinations and admission streams. Eligibility depends on age, prior qualification and the current official prospectus.

Admission route checklist

Confirm your class level, target examination session, stream, subject combination, identity documents and previous qualification records before payment or submission.

How our counselling helps

We help you understand the process, prepare documents and track your academy application. Official admission decisions and dates remain under NIOS.

Official-information reminder

Rules, fees and dates can change. Confirm them on the relevant official board or university website before submitting or paying.

Frequently asked questions

Is NIOS Best Academy the official NIOS website?

No. We are an independent education and counselling provider. Official admissions and rules are controlled by NIOS.

Which admission dates should I follow?

Always confirm the current window and late-fee dates on the official NIOS portal before submitting an application.
