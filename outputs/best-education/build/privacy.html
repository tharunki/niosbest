Privacy notice

TK's SOLUTION collects only the information needed to provide study materials, keep purchases secure and improve the service responsibly.

Your information

What we collect and why.

Purchase information

When secure checkout is enabled, we collect the email address and optional name you provide, plus the payment/order status supplied by the payment provider. We use this to verify a purchase, provide support and protect access to paid materials.

Content reports

If you report a problem with a study resource, we store the message and the material it refers to so our team can correct it. Please do not put passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a report.

Limited service analytics

We record privacy-preserving aggregate visit counts to understand whether the library is being used. We honour browser Do Not Track signals and do not sell personal information or use analytics to build advertising profiles.

How information is handled

Payment processing is handled by the payment provider you choose at checkout. TK's SOLUTION does not store card, UPI or bank credentials. Access to purchase and support records is limited to authorised TK's SOLUTION administrators.

We retain purchase records for support, accounting and legal obligations. We delete or minimise technical rate-limit and aggregate-analytics data on a regular schedule. You may ask about the personal information associated with a purchase, or ask us to correct it, by emailing sumathynl.maths@gmail.com.
