Prior qualification

Senior Secondary admission generally requires a recognised Secondary qualification; verify board-specific acceptance and gap rules officially.

Science and practicals

Science combinations can include practical subjects. Plan laboratory preparation, practical files and examination attendance early.

University planning

Check the eligibility rules of your intended university or course before locking subjects, especially Mathematics and science prerequisites.

Official-information reminder

Rules, fees and dates can change. Confirm them on the relevant official board or university website before submitting or paying.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does NIOS offer fixed Science, Commerce and Arts streams?

NIOS provides subject choice within its rules. “Streams” are commonly used guidance groupings rather than rigid school sections.

Will universities accept NIOS?

Recognition and course eligibility depend on the institution and programme; confirm directly with the target institution.
