Block 1 planning

Block 1 is commonly associated with an April/May public examination target. Confirm current opening and closing dates officially.

Block 2 planning

Block 2 is commonly associated with an October/November public examination target. Allow time for study, TMA and practical preparation.

Choose by readiness

The best block is the one that provides enough preparation time for your subject load and practical commitments.

Official-information reminder

Rules, fees and dates can change. Confirm them on the relevant official board or university website before submitting or paying.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are admission dates identical every year?

No. Dates, late-fee phases and rules can change; always verify the current official notice.

Can I switch blocks after admission?

Do not assume a switch is possible. Ask NIOS or your accredited institution before payment.
